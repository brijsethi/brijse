# AI Fluency

A focus area the author is building deliberately — both to develop his own fluency and
to teach it. Two strands now have material:

- **A teaching frame** — [[wiki/pages/ai-learning-modes]] plots effort against actual
  understanding to ask the question the author cares about: is the learner still doing
  the thinking? ([[wiki/sources/ai-fluency-teaching-modes]])
- **What comes first** — [[wiki/pages/ai-fluency-prerequisites]] gathers the language,
  reasoning, ambiguity-tolerance and "orchestration proxy" skills that may precede AI
  fluency, sharpened for school-age learners in India.
  ([[wiki/sources/ai-fluency-prerequisites-schools]])

[[wiki/pages/my-ai-setup]] documents the concrete tools and conventions the author works
with today.
