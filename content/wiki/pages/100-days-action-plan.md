# 100 Days Action Plan

A recurring personal structure: commit to **one definitive change**, and give it a
fixed 100-day window to implement, rather than an open-ended intention. Shows up
independently in two otherwise-unrelated notes:

- As a proposed method for sustaining motivation in college/early career — "single
  definitive change + 100 days of implementation" ([[wiki/sources/exploratory-writing]])
- As one of the concrete practices under "head → get things done," alongside staying
  undistracted and tracking daily tasks ([[wiki/sources/heheha]])

The repetition across unrelated notes suggests this is a load-bearing habit/structure
for the author, not a one-off idea.
