# HeHeHa Framework

A personal operating framework: **Head, Heart, Hands** — "used well."

- **Head**: creating content (notes, AI-assisted drafting, publishing) and getting
  things done (100-day projects, see [[wiki/pages/100-days-action-plan]]; daily task
  tracking; staying undistracted)
- **Heart**: prayer practice (Japa, Ardas, Sewa) and care (of self, of strong ties, of
  purpose)
- **Hands**: process work (persistence, strong ties, situational specificity) and
  energy work (cleanse, energize, recharge)
